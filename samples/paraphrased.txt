Computers can pick up patterns from examples instead of following hand-written rules, and this idea sits at the heart of modern AI. In healthcare, such algorithms help clinicians estimate which patients are in danger, spot illness on X-rays and scans, and suggest therapies suited to each person. The more examples a system sees, the better it performs, supporting quicker and more reliable choices by physicians. Because health records are private, hospitals must safeguard confidentiality and test models thoroughly before putting them into everyday use.
